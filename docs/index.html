Stress & emotions

Tracking young adults’ stress and emotions several times a day showed that stress and feelings push each other up and down over hours, and that highly stressed people get less relief from positive moods.

Le et al. (2026) Read more →

Lab head

Joshua F. Wiley

Associate Professor, School of Psychological Sciences, Monash University

Dr Joshua Wiley is an Associate Professor in the School of Psychological Sciences at Monash University and an Honorary Research Fellow at the Peter MacCallum Cancer Centre. He completed a PhD in health psychology and behavioural medicine at the University of California, Los Angeles, followed by postdoctoral training in randomised controlled trials.

His research follows how sleep, stress and emotions unfold day to day, then uses what it finds to build accessible sleep and emotion-regulation treatments. His group pairs data from phones and wearables with modern statistics to tailor what each person receives and when, and shares its methods as free software. The work is funded by the NHMRC, MRFF, ARC, US National Institutes of Health, US Department of Defense and the Wellcome Trust.
